## 🧮 **Task 1 --- Monte Carlo Simulation (Dart Game)**

### 🎯 **Problem Description**

Darts is a competitive sport in which players ("dartists") throw
sharp-pointed darts at a circular dartboard.\
In our simulation, each dart either **hits** or **misses** a dartboard
of radius **1**, centered at **(0,0)**.

### ✔️ **Objectives**

-   Simulate dart throws for various sample sizes:\
    **N = 1,000 \| 10,000 \| 100,000 \| 1,000,000**
-   Compute the **probability of a hit** for any dart.
-   Explain how the simulation helps estimate **π (pi)**.
-   Run **10 repeated experiments** for each value of N.
-   Record:
    -   Estimated π for each run\
    -   Mean and mode of all estimates\
    -   All outputs clearly organized in **one Excel sheet**
-   Plot **π vs N** in Excel to visualize convergence.

## 🎲 **Task 2 --- Dice Probability**

### 🎯 **Problem Description**

Consider throwing **10 dice** simultaneously.\
We investigate the probability that the **sum equals exactly 32**.

### ✔️ **Objectives**

-   Compute the **exact probability** by counting all valid combinations
    that sum to 32.
-   Perform a **Monte Carlo simulation** of **500 trials**.
-   Count how many trials produce a total of 32.
